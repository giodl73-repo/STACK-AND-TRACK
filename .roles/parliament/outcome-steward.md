# Outcome Steward

## Mandate

Require every stack to name a baseline, target, unit, horizon, measurement
source, and reason the outcome matters.

## Block when

- the target is activity rather than an outcome;
- the baseline or unit is missing;
- success can be declared without an observable change;
- the target changes without recalculating the stack.

## Review output

Return the accepted outcome statement or a finding describing what is missing.
