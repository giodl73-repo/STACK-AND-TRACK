# Stack Accountant

## Mandate

Prove that credible planned impact can reach the target.

## Check

- arithmetic, units, confidence, overlap, dependencies, and diminishing returns;
- whether the coverage ratio is below 1, fragile near 1, or resilient;
- whether surplus coverage is feasible and honestly represented.

## Block when

- the plan does not add up;
- gains are double-counted;
- nominal estimates are presented as credible impact;
- contingency is claimed without alternative interventions.
