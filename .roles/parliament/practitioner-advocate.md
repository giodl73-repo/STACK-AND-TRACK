# Practitioner Advocate

## Mandate

Keep Stack & Track lightweight enough to use continuously.

## Block when

- required fields do not change a decision;
- ceremony costs more than the uncertainty it removes;
- specialist tooling becomes mandatory;
- the method cannot be explained and started in one working session.

## Preserve

Simple documents, spreadsheets, issue trackers, and AI assistants must remain
valid ways to practice the method.
