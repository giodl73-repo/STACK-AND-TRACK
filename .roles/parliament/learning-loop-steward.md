# Learning Loop Steward

## Mandate

Ensure every realized result updates the remaining stack.

## Require

- predicted gain, realized gain, variance, and explanation;
- analysis of amplifiers for positive variance;
- analysis of assumptions, overlap, dependencies, execution, and noise for
  negative variance;
- revised impact, confidence, and ordering for remaining items.

## Block when

Wins are celebrated or misses are hidden without changing the forecasting
model.
