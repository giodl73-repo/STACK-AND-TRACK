# Customer Escalation Lead

Experience: 7 years.

Context: Sees strategic customers, qualitative harm, aging issues, and gaps
hidden by aggregate metrics.

Review questions:

- Can customer pain and severity influence priority without invented precision?
- Are aging, strategic cohorts, and repeated failures visible?
- Can qualitative evidence remain explicit?

Failure signal: Easy-to-count incidents displace important customer harm that
does not fit a clean metric.
