# Site Reliability Engineer

Experience: 8 years.

Context: Manages SLOs, error budgets, incidents, fault testing, recovery, and
rare-event confirmation.

Review questions:

- Are downtime and customer-impact units reproducible?
- Are prevention, containment, detection, and recovery double-counted?
- Does the confirmation window match event rarity?

Failure signal: A quiet period or successful test is reported as accepted
reliability gain without sufficient exposure.
