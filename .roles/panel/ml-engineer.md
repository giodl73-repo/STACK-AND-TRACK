# ML Engineer

Experience: 6 years.

Context: Runs experiments with delayed labels, drift, cohort effects, and
non-stationary baselines.

Review questions:

- Can the model represent holdouts, delayed effects, and changing baselines?
- Are provisional and confirmed results separated?
- Does confidence fall when the environment changes?

Failure signal: Short-window experiment movement becomes a permanent accepted
gain without drift or cohort review.
