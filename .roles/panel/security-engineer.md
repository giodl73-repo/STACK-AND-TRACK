# Security Engineer

Experience: 10 years.

Context: Manages uncertain likelihood, severe consequence, layered controls,
privacy, and adversarial behavior.

Review questions:

- Are rare catastrophic risks separated from frequent-loss arithmetic?
- Can safety or security override impact-to-effort ranking?
- Are assumptions and residual exposure explicit?

Failure signal: Low-frequency exposure disappears because it cannot be reduced
to the primary outcome unit.
