# Prompt: Build a Stack

```text
You are helping build a Stack & Track impact portfolio.

Follow AI_GUIDE.md, METHODOLOGY.md, PRINCIPLES.md, and GLOSSARY.md.

Rules:
- Do not invent measurements, owners, dates, or accepted facts.
- Label every material value measured, human-provided, calculated, proposed,
  or unknown.
- AI-generated estimates are proposed.
- Unknown values remain unknown, not zero.
- Do not treat activities or shipment as realized impact.
- Preserve safety, quality, cost, tail, cohort, privacy, and externality
  constraints.

Input:
[Paste the goal, evidence, constraints, and any existing candidates.]

Produce:

1. Outcome statement
   - metric
   - baseline
   - target
   - required impact
   - unit
   - horizon
   - measurement source
   - outcome owner
   - guardrails

2. Missing-information ledger
   For each missing field, explain why it matters and who or what can resolve it.

3. Candidate stack
   For each candidate:
   - ID and title
   - mechanism
   - predicted impact and source label
   - confidence and basis
   - credible impact method
   - effort
   - time to learning
   - dependencies
   - overlap
   - owner
   - measurement plan
   - guardrail risk

4. Coverage
   - required impact
   - nominal impact
   - confidence adjustment
   - overlap and dependency adjustment
   - credible impact
   - Stack Coverage Ratio
   - sensitivity

5. Pareto proposal
   Propose, but do not approve, a smallest credible first portfolio. Preserve
   alternatives and contingency.

6. Timing proposal
   Place candidates into learning-now, landing-soon, structural-later, and
   contingency lanes. Identify the earliest trustworthy evidence date for each.

7. Findings
   Return pass, finding, or defer for outcome clarity, stack sufficiency,
   attribution readiness, guardrails, and measurement readiness.

End with explicit human decisions required before commitment.
```
