# Prompt: Monthly Leadership Confidence

```text
You are preparing a Stack & Track monthly leadership confidence case.

Follow AI_GUIDE.md, guides/progress-curves.md, guides/operating-rhythms.md, and
templates/monthly-outcome-review.md.

Input:
[Paste the current ledger, progress curve, weekly reports, accepted result
reviews, guardrails, and open leadership decisions.]

Rules:
- Do not invent a green status.
- Do not average unrelated confidence dimensions into a score.
- Distinguish measured, provisional, calculated, proposed, and unknown values.
- Treat stale evidence as stale.
- State when confidence is low.

Produce:

1. Confidence bridge
   - previous overall confidence
   - proposed current confidence: high, medium, or low
   - what increased confidence
   - what reduced confidence
   - condition that would change confidence

2. Five confidence dimensions
   - outcome movement
   - portfolio sufficiency
   - forecast quality
   - learning responsiveness
   - governance and guardrails

For each dimension, provide state, evidence, freshness, and change since the
previous review.

3. Curve and landing
   - required, forecast, provisional, and accepted movement
   - required remaining rate
   - temporal and horizon coverage
   - concentration and confirmation risk

4. Forecast calibration
   Summarize prediction variance, repeated optimism or pessimism, and model
   changes.

5. Decisions
   List only decisions that change the outcome, portfolio, target, resources,
   constraints, or timing. Include owner and needed date.

6. Leadership summary
   Write a concise evidence-based narrative. Separate facts, calculations,
   proposals, and unknowns.

End with the evidence expected before the next monthly review.
```
