# Prompt: Re-stack

```text
You are applying accepted Stack & Track learning to the remaining portfolio.

Follow AI_GUIDE.md, METHODOLOGY.md, and .roles/ROLE.md.

Input:
[Paste the accepted result review, current ledger, progress curve, constraints,
and open decisions.]

Rules:
- Preserve original predictions and history.
- Use only accepted realized gain when calculating the current outcome.
- Keep provisional results separate.
- Do not add positive variance twice.
- Do not remove guardrails to recover schedule.
- Proposed changes require human acceptance.

Produce:

1. Current state
   - goal version
   - accepted outcome
   - denominator exposed
   - outstanding impact
   - remaining time
   - required remaining rate

2. Learning propagation
   For each related candidate:
   - previous impact
   - proposed revised impact
   - previous confidence
   - proposed confidence
   - overlap or dependency change
   - timing change
   - reason and evidence

3. Revised coverage
   - credible remaining impact
   - Stack Coverage Ratio
   - Temporal Coverage Ratio by checkpoint
   - Horizon Coverage Ratio
   - sensitivity
   - exposure-weighted realization by checkpoint

4. Revised Pareto order
   Explain accelerations, deferrals, retirements, and new candidates.

5. Portfolio lanes
   Rebalance learning-now, landing-soon, structural-later, and contingency work.

6. Decisions
   List the human decisions required, accountable owner, needed date, and
   consequence of delay.

7. Findings
   Return pass, finding, or defer for sufficiency, timing, attribution,
   guardrails, and leadership confidence.

If the trigger is a material goal or technology change, first produce a transfer
audit, preserve the prior goal history, establish the new baseline, and require
human acceptance of the revised commitment.
```
