# Public Publication and Abstraction Policy

STACK-AND-TRACK is a public methodology repository. Private workplace evidence
may inform the method, but private organizations, products, projects, teams,
people, customers, systems, documents, conversations, links, and operational
details do not belong in this repository.

## Public-safe rule

Publish the **generalizable mechanism and arithmetic**, not the private source.

Use:

- generic domains such as web services, mobile synchronization, authoring
  tools, reliability, customer experience, cost, or incident management;
- synthetic, rounded, normalized, or deliberately transformed numbers;
- fictional intervention names;
- generic rollout stages and population cohorts;
- disclosed simulations and illustrative examples;
- public sources that can be cited independently.

Do not publish:

- employer, customer, tenant, product, project, feature, team, or internal
  program names;
- names of workplace participants, except credited methodology creators who
  have agreed to public attribution;
- internal document, meeting, email, chat, recording, dashboard, work-item, or
  file titles;
- private links, identifiers, paths, screenshots, or copied excerpts;
- exact confidential baselines, targets, populations, dates, roadmaps,
  incidents, regressions, or results;
- details that become identifying when combined, even if each detail appears
  harmless alone;
- claims that a private organization endorses or formally adopted the method.

## Number transformation

Numbers may be used publicly only when they are:

1. already public and cited;
2. explicitly approved for publication; or
3. transformed into a synthetic example.

A synthetic transformation should preserve the lesson while breaking source
identity:

| Preserve | Transform |
|---|---|
| additive, multiplicative, bounded, or exposure-weighted relationship | exact baseline and target |
| underperformance, positive variance, regression, or rollback shape | exact intervention and feature names |
| rollout concentration and timing lesson | exact regions, stations, dates, and release trains |
| coverage, confidence, overlap, and contingency behavior | exact internal estimates and realized values |
| leadership or capacity decision pattern | organization, team, owner, and roadmap |

Changing only a product name is not enough. Alter the surrounding numbers,
timing, intervention labels, and identifying combination of facts.

## Public example labels

Every non-publicly sourced example must state one of:

- **Synthetic example:** invented to demonstrate the method.
- **Transformed composite:** combines and alters multiple patterns so it does
  not describe a specific private case.
- **Public case study:** grounded only in cited public sources.

Do not call a transformed example a historical case study.

## Generic use-case naming

Prefer:

- web-service latency;
- page or application startup;
- mobile synchronization;
- authoring or editing responsiveness;
- API reliability;
- deployment safety;
- customer satisfaction;
- cost per transaction;
- engineering toil;
- incident-impact minutes;
- technology migration.

Avoid internal brand, organization, architecture, project, or feature names.

## AI handling

When workplace search or AI tools surface private material:

1. use it only to identify general patterns;
2. do not copy text, titles, links, identifiers, or distinctive facts into the
   public repository;
3. create a synthetic or transformed example from the pattern;
4. run a re-identification review before publication;
5. keep private provenance outside the public repository.

## Re-identification review

Before publishing, ask:

- Could an employee identify the source project from this combination of
  metric, date, rollout, intervention, and result?
- Does the example reproduce a distinctive internal event or sequence?
- Does any link, title, identifier, person, customer, or product remain?
- Are exact private numbers unnecessary to teach the lesson?
- Is the example clearly labeled synthetic, transformed, or public?

If the answer creates doubt, transform further or do not publish.
