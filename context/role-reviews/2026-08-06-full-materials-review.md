# Full Materials Role Review

Date: 2026-08-06

## Scope

This review covers the complete public repository corpus:

- front-door and canonical doctrine;
- guides and templates;
- core and transformed-composite examples;
- prompts and optional portable model;
- simulations;
- publication policy;
- prior role-review records and indexes.

The review tests material coherence and navigation. It does not substitute for
independent practitioner testing.

## Dissent and limits

The synthetic Junior Engineer and Practitioner Advocate pass the navigational
structure, but both defer the empirical question of whether an unfamiliar
person can complete the method without coaching.

The Stack Accountant and Numeracy Checker pass inspectable arithmetic, but do
not claim that any proposed estimate formula is universally valid.

The Scope Keeper preserves the optional model and prompts but rejects software
implementation until repeated human use demonstrates an unmet need.

## Disposition

No critical or major actionable role finding remains. The full materials corpus
is accepted at synthetic role-review fixed point.

The independent-human self-service usability gate remains open. The README is
good enough to use as the participant's starting surface for that test.
