## Parliament review

| Role | Result | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Outcome Steward | pass | The quick start and canvas require baseline, target, unit, horizon, measurement source, owner, and constraints. |
| Stack Accountant | pass | All three examples reproduce their required-impact and coverage arithmetic and distinguish nominal from credible impact. |
| Pareto Prioritizer | pass | Examples select high-contribution portfolios while retaining alternatives and explaining why frequency or ease alone does not control priority. |
| Attribution Skeptic | pass | Initial drafts could be read as independently additive per-item claims. They now make the authoritative portfolio movement primary and treat item gains as a reconciling attribution model. |
| Learning Loop Steward | pass | Templates and examples require variance explanation, transferable learning, forecast revision, and re-stacking. |
| Constraint Realist | pass | The reliability example rejects impossible literal 2x coverage and uses loss units, scenarios, and independent paths. |
| Practitioner Advocate | defer | Content is concise and tool-neutral, but the required independent quick-start usability session has not yet occurred. Destination: Release 0.1 usability review. |
