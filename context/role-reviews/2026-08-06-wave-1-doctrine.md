## Parliament review

| Role | Result | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Outcome Steward | pass | Principles and definitions begin with a measurable outcome and prohibit activity-only success. |
| Stack Accountant | pass | Doctrine distinguishes nominal and credible impact, requires inspectable adjustments, and reports sensitivity below 1x as fragility. |
| Pareto Prioritizer | pass | Priority includes contribution, confidence, effort, time to learning, safety, and constraints while preserving alternatives. |
| Attribution Skeptic | pass | Portfolio movement remains authoritative and per-item attribution must reconcile without overlap inflation. |
| Learning Loop Steward | pass | Variance explanation and propagation are separate explicit principles. |
| Constraint Realist | pass | Surplus coverage is preferred but bounded metrics, floors, ceilings, and nonlinear systems prevent dogmatic 2x use. |
| Practitioner Advocate | pass | The doctrine is organized into short principles, canonical terms, and practical questions rather than a new ceremony. |
