# Adoption Materials Role Review

Date: 2026-08-06

Reviewed artifacts:

- `guides/first-working-session.md`
- `guides/usability-test.md`
- `guides/adoption-maturity.md`
- `templates/practitioner-feedback.md`

## Parliament review

| Role | Result | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Outcome Steward | pass | Adoption success is defined as correct use and diagnosis, not whether the participant produces an execution-ready stack. |
| Stack Accountant | pass | Participants must calculate coverage or explicitly identify insufficient information; facilitators cannot rescue arithmetic with unsupported estimates. |
| Pareto Prioritizer | pass | The facilitated session builds broadly, tests sufficiency, then selects and staggers a smallest credible portfolio. |
| Attribution Skeptic | pass | Usability tasks require participants to distinguish shipment, provisional gain, accepted gain, and re-stacking after variance. |
| Learning Loop Steward | pass | Every observed confusion becomes a finding with artifact, consequence, owner, and closure condition. |
| Constraint Realist | pass | Valid session outcomes include insufficient coverage, unclear targets, and blocked measurement rather than forced commitment. |
| Practitioner Advocate | pass | The self-service test measures whether the materials work without verbal reconstruction, while the separate facilitator guide supports adoption. |

## Stakeholder review

| Role | Result | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Outcome Owner | pass | A real target remains owned by the accountable participant and can be declared unclear rather than completed artificially. |
| Delivery Team | pass | Sessions preserve unknowns and distinguish discovery from execution commitment. |
| Operator | pass | Safety or confidentiality can stop a test, and facilitated sessions retain guardrail and recovery challenges. |
| Affected User | pass | Public reports must anonymize people, targets, customer information, and proprietary details. |
| AI Practitioner | pass | AI values remain proposed during facilitated work, and the self-service test measures the repository rather than hidden AI coaching. |

## Assurance review

| Role | Result | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Goodhart Auditor | pass | Maturity cannot be raised through document count, dashboards, automation, unsupported coverage, or frequent evidence-free reprioritization. |
| Safety and Externalities Steward | pass | Privacy, confidentiality, safety stops, and non-public artifacts are explicit. |

## Editorial review

| Role | Result | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Numeracy Checker | pass | Test observations capture timing, arithmetic errors, coverage decisions, and unresolved findings without converting them into a synthetic usability score. |
| Scope Keeper | pass | The maturity model evaluates a specific outcome practice, not employees, organizations, procurement eligibility, or overall worth. |
| Clarity Editor | pass | Self-service testing, facilitated adoption, feedback, release gates, and maturity have separate purposes and artifacts. |

## Finding ledger

| Severity | Finding | Owner | Closure |
|---|---|---|---|
| major | Observer coaching could make the usability test pass even when the materials were incomplete | maintainer | Closed by limiting observers to navigation, recording, and safety while treating method assistance as a finding |
| major | A maturity model could become a vanity score or employee evaluation | maintainer | Closed by evidence-based levels, explicit anti-gaming rules, outcome-specific assessment, and prohibited uses |
| major | Real usability artifacts could expose confidential outcomes, customers, or operational details | maintainer | Closed through anonymization, retention limits, explicit permission, and safety/confidentiality stop rules |
