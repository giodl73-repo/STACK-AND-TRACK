# Synthetic Corporate Practitioner Panel

Date: 2026-08-06

## Disclosure

This is an AI-simulated review using the 12 synthetic corporate roles in
`.roles/panel/`. No role represents a real employee or claims real-world
experience. The simulation does not close the independent-human Release 0.1
usability gate.

## Review task

Each role attempted to reason through:

1. creating an outcome statement;
2. building and estimating a stack;
3. testing coverage;
4. selecting and staggering work;
5. measuring and reviewing results;
6. re-stacking;
7. explaining monthly leadership confidence.

## Finding ledger

| Severity | Finding | Raised by | Closure |
|---|---|---|---|
| major | Materials appeared to prefer point estimates even when ranges or scenarios were more honest | Junior, Backend, Principal | Closed by adding point/range/scenario guidance to `QUICKSTART.md` and the stack canvas |
| major | "Authoritative source" did not provide a measurement-quality review | Data Platform, ML, SRE | Closed by adding `guides/measurement-quality.md` and a canvas disposition |
| major | Multiple metrics could be forced into a false common unit | Frontend, Security, Customer Escalation | Closed by adding `guides/multi-metric-outcomes.md` |
| major | Surplus coverage could become excessive concurrent work | Engineering Manager, TPM, SRE | Closed by adding available capacity and safe WIP limits to the canvas |

| minor | A dedicated ML example should cover drift, holdouts, and delayed labels | ML Engineer, Data Platform Engineer | Defer to the example backlog |
| minor | A financial benefit example should separate gross, net, recurring, and shifted cost | Product Manager, Engineering Manager | Defer to the example backlog |

## Cross-panel conclusions

Strongest material:

- quick-start sequence;
- shipment versus accepted gain;
- progress curves and confidence bridge;
- high-reliability and fundamentals examples;
- explicit unknown and proposed source labels.

Most important corrections:

- allow ranges and scenarios;
- qualify measurement quality;
- prevent false multi-metric composites;
- separate portfolio coverage from work-in-progress capacity.

## Disposition

No critical or major simulated finding remains after the documented corrections.
The three minor items remain in the material backlog.

The real independent-human usability gate remains `defer`.
