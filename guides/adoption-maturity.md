# Adoption Maturity

This model describes observable practice, not organizational worth. Do not turn
it into a vanity score, employee rating, or procurement requirement.

## Levels

### Level 0: Target only

The team has a goal or metric but no visible intervention-impact model.

Signals:

- roadmap or backlog presented as the plan;
- proposed gains do not add up;
- shipment treated as progress.

Next practice: create a stack canvas.

### Level 1: Visible stack

The team lists candidates, predicted impact, confidence, ownership, and
measurement.

Signals:

- assumptions and unknowns are visible;
- alternatives remain in the stack;
- coverage may still be nominal or fragile.

Next practice: adjust for overlap, dependencies, and confidence.

### Level 2: Credible portfolio

The team tests outcome and temporal sufficiency and Pareto-selects a balanced
portfolio.

Signals:

- Stack Coverage Ratio is inspectable;
- learning-now, landing-soon, structural-later, and contingency lanes exist;
- build through confirmation dates are visible.

Next practice: accept only measured results.

### Level 3: Measured realization

The team separates shipment, provisional gain, and accepted realized gain.

Signals:

- portfolio movement is authoritative;
- attribution reconciles to measured movement;
- guardrails and affected users remain visible.

Next practice: make variance change the remaining stack.

### Level 4: Learning portfolio

Every meaningful result updates assumptions, confidence, overlap, timing, and
priority across related candidates.

Signals:

- misses produce visible model changes;
- positive variance is not counted twice;
- re-stacking occurs while time remains.

Next practice: calibrate forecasting and governance.

### Level 5: Calibrated operating system

The organization improves the method from accumulated prediction, realization,
timeliness, and decision evidence.

Signals:

- confidence levels are calibrated against history;
- recurring estimation and overlap errors are corrected;
- leadership closes outcome-changing decisions promptly;
- unnecessary fields and meetings are retired;
- software is considered only for repeated proven friction.

Next practice: continue learning; there is no permanent completion state.

## Assess by evidence

For each claimed level, cite:

- current artifacts;
- recent accepted results;
- examples of learning propagation;
- forecast calibration;
- leadership decisions;
- unresolved findings.

An organization may operate at different levels for different outcomes. Report
the level of the specific practice, not an average enterprise score.

## Anti-gaming rules

- More documents do not increase maturity.
- A dashboard does not prove measured realization.
- Automation does not prove calibration.
- High coverage based on unsupported estimates is not Level 2.
- Frequent re-prioritization without evidence is not Level 4.
- Leadership confidence without transparent misses is not Level 5.
